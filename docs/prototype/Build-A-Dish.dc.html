Build-A-Dish · design notes · 23 Sep 2026

Five dishes. One shelf. One Bin.

A daily, city-shared cooking minigame in a fourth OraX tab. Everyone in Singapore or Kuala Lumpur cooks from the same Pantry. You commit to one dish a day, build it by tapping portions and stroking three loose sigils, and the Bin judges the plate in your own gem.

Kept from the docs

  • The Bin as judge, centre stage. Dry, judgemental, the payoff beat of every plate.
  • Always produce something: no fail state, procedural cursed names, a Cursed Plates gallery.
  • Roles, states and dish families drive recognition and naming.
  • The ingredient book becomes the shared Pantry.
  • Sigils, reduced to three gesture families: slash cuts, spiral heats, flick plates.
  • Signature Dish on the profile. Mess and wipe survive as an optional toy.

Cut or parked

  • Unity 2.5D room, physics, soft landing, slingshot disposal. Web-native 2D instead.
  • Wand and Culinary Focus: flavour text only.
  • QTE timing bars, drag-and-drop plating, three class origin stories.

New in this pass

  • Today's dishes: five, Asian and Western, with live cooking counts and avatars.
  • City Pantry with Running low and Gone states. Soft cap of three portions.
  • Leftovers hour, 21:00 to midnight city time: caps lift on anything still plentiful. Ten portions of ginger sauce, legally.
  • Same-dish wall sorted by the Bin's rating, most cursed at the bottom, plus a same-dish thread.
  • Share card: dish, verdict, class avatar, gem, stones, city and date.

Assumptions

  • Stones are a hidden 0 to 100 score shown in your gem: 75 and up is three, 45 and up is two. Cursed means under 35, two extras, or two burnt items.
  • Habit tags come from repeated behaviour: doubling the chilli, raw rice, unhinged portions.
  • Art is placeholders. Drop the Bin and dish renders into the slots.
  • The countdown runs on the real UTC+8 clock. Force Leftovers hour from Tweaks.

Try: Dish tab, pick chicken rice, tap ginger sauce four times, spiral over the chicken three times, flick up. Then open Cursed Plates.

Next: real Bin and dish art, stroke tuning on device, a backend for the pool and counts, thread moderation.
